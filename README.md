Individual Case Safety Report (ICSR)

1. Introduction

An Individual Case Safety Report (ICSR) is a structured report containing information about a suspected adverse event or adverse drug reaction in an individual patient. ICSRs support pharmacovigilance activities and medicine safety monitoring.

This educational project follows the general concepts of the ICH E2B(R3) ICSR framework.

2. Objectives

- Identify a suspected adverse drug reaction.
- Document patient and medicine information.
- Assess the relationship between the medicine and reaction.
- Classify severity, seriousness, and expectedness.
- Prepare a structured educational safety report.
